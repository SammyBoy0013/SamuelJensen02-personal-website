Bachelor project · with Aaveq Robotics

Modular Encasement Platform for Scalable Testing Solutions

A waterproof, modular electronics enclosure for autonomous unmanned surface vessels operating in harsh marine conditions, including Greenland.

[Hero image — the prototype enclosure, open, showing the pegboard levels]
[CAD assembly]
[Water-ingress test]

Problem

Electronics on board were poorly protected from water, damage was hard to assess after a mission, and servicing or reconfiguring the vessel took far too long.

Approach

A Stage-Gate-inspired process with the company: user journey mapping, modular product-family theory, a module identification matrix, then iterative cardboard and 3D-printed prototypes in full scale.

Result

A prototype with standardised mounting plates and tool-free clips on configurable levels, reaching IPX7 water resistance.
